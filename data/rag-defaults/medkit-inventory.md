# Basic Medkit Inventory

This is a basic first-aid kit reference for MedisinACSHS. It explains what each item is generally for. Follow the product label and approved first-aid guidance. This list does not replace professional medical care. Call 911 for an emergency.

## Wound Care and Cleaning

### Adhesive bandages / Band-Aid
Purpose: Cover small, minor cuts and scrapes (maliit na sugat at gasgas). Clean the area first and replace the bandage when wet or dirty.

### Sterile gauze pads
Purpose: Cover wounds and help control bleeding (pagtakip sa sugat at bleeding control). For heavy bleeding, apply firm direct pressure and seek emergency help.

### Medical adhesive tape
Purpose: Secure gauze, dressings, or bandages in place (pang-secure ng gauze). Do not wrap tape tightly around a limb.

### Antiseptic solution or wipes
Purpose: Clean the skin around a minor wound (paglilinis sa paligid ng minor wound). Follow the product label and avoid getting the product in the eyes unless it is specifically made for that use.

### Saline solution
Purpose: Rinse the eyes or flush minor wounds (pagbanlaw ng mata o minor wounds). Use sterile saline intended for wound or eye rinsing and seek medical help for an eye injury or vision change.

### Cotton swabs
Purpose: Minor cleaning or applying a product to a small area (minor cleaning/application). Do not insert cotton swabs into the ear, eye, or a deep wound.

### Burn dressing / sterile non-stick dressing
Purpose: Cover minor burns without sticking to the injured skin (minor burns). For serious, deep, extensive, electrical, chemical, or facial burns, seek emergency care.

## Hygiene and Protection

### Alcohol or hand sanitizer
Purpose: Hand hygiene before providing first aid (hand hygiene bago mag-first aid). Use hand sanitizer when soap and water are unavailable; allow it to dry and keep it away from flames.

### Disposable gloves
Purpose: Protect the rescuer and patient from contact with blood or body fluids (protection ng rescuer at patient). Put gloves on before first aid and dispose of them safely afterward.

### Face masks
Purpose: Basic infection control and reduction of respiratory droplet exposure. Use according to local health guidance and replace a wet or soiled mask.

### CPR face shield / barrier
Purpose: Provide a barrier during CPR and rescue breathing (protection during CPR). Use it according to its instructions and call emergency services before starting emergency care.

## Sprains, Bumps, and Support

### Instant cold pack / instant cold compress
Purpose: Apply cold for minor bumps, sprains, injuries, and swelling (minor bumps, sprains, minor injury, or swelling). Use a cloth barrier, follow the package directions, and do not apply directly to bare skin for prolonged periods.

### Elastic bandage
Purpose: Provide basic support or compression for a minor sprain (basic support/compression for minor sprains). It should be snug, not tight; loosen it if there is numbness, tingling, increasing pain, or color change.

### Triangular bandage
Purpose: Make a sling or provide basic support (sling/support). Seek medical assessment for severe pain, deformity, or inability to move the injured area.

### Emergency blanket
Purpose: Help maintain body warmth (pagpapanatili ng body warmth), especially while waiting for emergency help. Do not use it to cover a person who is actively overheating.

## Tools and Monitoring

### Safety scissors
Purpose: Cut gauze, medical tape, or bandages safely (pagputol ng gauze, tape, o bandage). Keep scissors away from the skin when cutting clothing or dressings.

### Tweezers
Purpose: Remove a superficial splinter or other superficial foreign material (pagkuha ng superficial splinter/foreign material). Do not dig into skin or remove an object embedded in the eye, neck, chest, or another serious wound.

### Digital thermometer
Purpose: Check body temperature (pag-check ng temperature). Follow the thermometer instructions, clean it as directed, and record the reading and time when monitoring illness.

## Escalation

The MedisinACSHS medkit is a primary first-aid resource. When a kit item is appropriate, recommend the actual item and give one concise action. The medkit is for basic first aid only. Get urgent professional help for severe or uncontrolled bleeding, trouble breathing, unconsciousness, suspected broken bones, serious burns, poisoning, severe allergic reaction, chest pain, stroke symptoms, or any rapidly worsening condition. Call 911 first for an emergency.
